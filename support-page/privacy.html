Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

At StudyPlan, your privacy matters. We are committed to protecting your personal information and ensuring transparency about how your data is collected, used, and stored.

Information We Collect

We may collect information that you voluntarily provide, including study tasks, deadlines, subjects, schedules, notes, and planner-related content. Account-related information may also be stored when required for authentication and account management.

How We Use Your Information

  • To organize and manage your study schedule.
  • To provide task tracking and planning features.
  • To improve platform functionality and user experience.
  • To maintain account security and data integrity.

AI-Powered Features

Content submitted through Smart Paste or AI extraction tools is processed solely for extracting academic tasks, deadlines, reminders, and study-related information. This data is not sold or shared for advertising purposes.

Data Storage & Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect your information from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or disclosure. While no system can guarantee absolute security, we continuously work to maintain a secure environment.

StudyPlan may use trusted third-party providers for hosting, authentication, analytics, and infrastructure services. These providers only receive access necessary to perform their services and are expected to handle data responsibly.

Your Rights

You may request access to your data, correction of inaccurate information, or deletion of your account data where applicable. Users remain in control of the information they create and store within the platform.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our services or legal requirements. Any updates will be posted on this page along with a revised "Last Updated" date.

Contact Us

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please visit our Support page for assistance.
